Privacy & responsible use

    
    
      
  • Local only. Cookie Vault has no server, no analytics and makes no network requests. Data leaves the browser only when you save or copy it.
    • 
      
  • Read-only. This extension never creates, edits or deletes cookies, and never replays them for you.
    • 
      
  • Treat exports like passwords. A cookies file can impersonate a logged-in session. Store it securely and delete it when you are finished. "Hide cookie values" produces a shareable, value-free audit.
    • 
      
  • Authorised use only. Inspect cookies for accounts and systems you own or have explicit written permission to test. Unauthorised access may violate laws such as the CFAA.
    • 
      
  • Permissions. cookies to read them, tabs to know the current page, downloads to save your file, storage to remember your options, clipboardWrite for the Copy button.
